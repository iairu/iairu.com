## Instructions

### Thin chicken breast pieces

1. Wash the chicken before cutting.
2. Cut it into thin pieces and put them in a large container.
3. Rinse again.
4. Cover with very lightly salted water.
5. Let sit while you crush the cornflakes.
6. Stir once more and pour off the water.

### Crushed cornflakes

- Crush softly under a blanket in a plastic bag (the bag is bound to get pinholes).
- Later in the process, use the bag to mix the cornflakes over the breasts.

### Marinade

Put the following ingredients on top of the pieces:

- ~3 tsp paprika powder
- ~1 tsp kebab seasoning
- ~2 tsp ginger powder
- red pepper flakes
- salt

Then:

1. Mix thoroughly.
2. Lightly sprinkle garlic powder over the top.
3. 3 lemon drops per cut piece.
4. Let rest.
5. While marinating, prepare everything for the frying process below.

### Egg wash

1. Mix one or two eggs.
2. Add 1.5 tsp of oregano.
3. Add a bit of paprika powder for colour.
4. Add red pepper flakes.
5. Mix again.

### Frying

Heat up oil in the frying pan, preferably close to deep-frying temperature. Lay out and apply in the following order:

1. all-purpose flour
2. egg wash
3. cornflake bag
4. frying pan
