## Equipment Comparison

### Graphic Tablets

I've worked with various graphic tablets over the years. Each has its strengths:

**Display Tablets**
- Direct drawing on screen
- More intuitive hand-eye coordination
- Higher price point
- Excellent for detailed work

**Standard Tablets**
- More affordable
- Portable and lightweight
- Requires practice for hand-eye coordination
- Great for beginners
