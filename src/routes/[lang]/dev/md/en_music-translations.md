# Music Lyric Translations

A collection of song lyrics translated between various languages, preserving both meaning and poetic flow.

## About Translations

Translating song lyrics is an art form that requires balancing:
- **Literal meaning**: Staying true to the original words
- **Poetic structure**: Maintaining rhythm and rhyme where possible
- **Cultural context**: Adapting cultural references appropriately
- **Emotional tone**: Preserving the feeling of the original

## Translation Philosophy

When translating music, I follow these principles:

1. **Respect the artist**: Honor the original creator's intent
2. **Context matters**: Consider the song's background and cultural setting
3. **Singability**: When possible, make translations singable to the original melody
4. **Footnotes**: Provide cultural context where needed
5. **Multiple versions**: Sometimes offer both literal and adapted translations
